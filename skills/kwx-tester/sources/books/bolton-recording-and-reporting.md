- Year: 2017

## What I read

The bug reporting, test reporting, coverage and safety language parts of the deck (about the first 40 slides of text).

## Key lessons used in exploratory/bug-reports.md and exploratory/exploratory.md

- "PEOPLE WORKing" checklist: Problem, Example, Oracle, Polite, Literate, Extrapolation, Workaround; the first three almost always needed.
- An oracle is "a means of recognizing a problem when it happens during testing"; it is heuristic and can fail.
- Kaner's RIMGEA quoted: maximise is "the biggest, baddest bug it could possibly be", generalise is where else it might manifest, externalise is what happens when it gets outside the lab.
- The significance of a problem may be proportional to the reasonableness of the workaround.
- Test case counts and pass rates mean nothing out of context; report problems, a coverage outline, time per area, a three part testing story (status, testing, value) and session reports.
- Coverage levels 0 to 3 (aware only, reconnaissance, core and critical, deep and harsh).
- Safety language: "so far", "apparently", "I infer", "I assumed", to avoid false confidence.
