## What I read

The publisher's free excerpt: "Good Charters" and "Generating Charters". The charter example boxes are images and did not extract. The rest of the book was not read (paid).

## Key lessons used in exploratory/exploratory.md

- The charter template "Explore (target) with (resources) to discover (information)" (chapter 2; widely quoted, the template box itself was an image in the excerpt).
- "A good charter offers direction without overspecifying test actions"; too specific becomes a test case, too broad never finishes.
- "A good charter is a prompt: it suggests sources of inspiration without dictating precise actions or outcomes."
- Charter sources: requirements discussions, questions that reveal uncertainty or dependencies, implicit expectations (security model, performance), stakeholder questions, existing artifacts (code comments, bug database, support logs), new discoveries during sessions.
- Check with stakeholders that they would act on the information before spending hours exploring.
