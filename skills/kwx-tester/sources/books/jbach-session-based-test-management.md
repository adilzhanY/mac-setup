# Session-Based Test Management

- Author: Jonathan Bach (with James Bach)
- Year: 2000 (Software Testing and Quality Engineering magazine, November/December 2000)
- Legal URL: https://www.satisfice.com/download/session-based-test-management (free PDF from the author's company)

## What I read

The full 6 page article, including the session sheet example, the metrics section and "Notes from the field".

## Key lessons used in exploratory/exploratory.md

- A session is "an uninterrupted block of reviewable, chartered test effort"; about 90 minutes, short about 45, long about 2 hours.
- Session sheet sections: charter (mission and areas), tester, start time, task breakdown (test design and execution, bug investigation and reporting, session setup: "TBS"), data files, test notes, issues, bugs; also charter versus opportunity.
- Bugs are concerns about product quality; issues are questions or problems about the test process or project.
- Debrief agenda PROOF: Past, Results, Obstacles, Outlook, Feelings.
- If the charter cannot be fulfilled, abort and start a different session; if most time went to opportunity, rewrite the charter to match.
- A queue of todo charters ("the hopper") lets the team prioritise requests against planned sessions.
- Metrics can be distorted by a confused or biased manager or tester; they need debriefs to stay honest.
