## Key lessons used in web/accessibility-testing.md

- 95.9% of the top million home pages had detected WCAG failures; 56.1 errors per page.
- Six failure types make 96% of errors: low contrast 83.9%, missing alt 53.1%, missing labels 51%, empty links 46.3%, empty buttons 30.6%, missing language 13.5%.
- Pages with ARIA averaged 59.1 errors vs 42 without; correlation, not proven cause.
