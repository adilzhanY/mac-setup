# Heuristic Test Strategy Model (HTSM)

- Author: James Bach (Satisfice, Inc.)
- Year: version 6.3, 5 November 2024 (copyright 1996 to 2024)
- Legal URL: https://www.satisfice.com/download/heuristic-test-strategy-model (free PDF, 5 pages)

## What I read

The whole PDF: the overview (project environment, product factors, quality criteria, general test techniques, the testing story), the nine general techniques, the project environment questions, the product factors (SFDPOT with all sub items) and the quality criteria categories.

## Key lessons used in exploratory/heuristics.md and exploratory/exploratory.md

- Product factors are Structure, Function, Data, Interfaces, Platform, Operations, Time (the "San Francisco Depot" mnemonic). v6.3 lists sub items such as Data: cardinality, big and little, invalid and noise, lifecycle; Operations: disfavored use; Time: pacing and concurrency.
- Nine general test techniques: function, claims, domain, user, stress, risk, flow, tool supported, scenario testing.
- Quality criteria are categories of risk: capability, reliability, usability, charisma, security, scalability, compatibility, performance, installability, development.
- "You can never know the actual quality of a software product" but you can tell a testing story from tests, including bugs and curios.
- "Software is complex and invisible. Take care to cover all of it that matters, not just the parts that are easy to see."
