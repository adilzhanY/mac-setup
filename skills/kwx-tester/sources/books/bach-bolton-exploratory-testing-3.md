## What I read

The first part of the post (definition, formality continuum, checking versus testing).

## Key lessons used in exploratory/exploratory.md

- "Testing is the process of evaluating a product by learning about it through exploration and experimentation", continuing with questioning, study, modelling, observation and inference.
- All testing is exploratory; scripting is a technique within it. The term "exploratory testing" was retired in Rapid Software Testing for that reason.
- The formality continuum runs from freestyle to fully scripted; "script" includes any outside control on the tester's choices.
- Checking (algorithmic output comparison) can be fully automated; testing cannot.
