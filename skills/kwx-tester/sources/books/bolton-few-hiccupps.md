## What I read

The full blog post.

## Key lessons used in exploratory/heuristics.md and exploratory/exploratory.md

- Oracles: Familiarity (absence of familiar problems), Explainability, World, History, Image, Comparable products, Claims, User expectations (later "user desires"), Product, Purpose, Statutes and standards.
- "Oracle principles are, like all heuristics, fallible and context-dependent; to be applied, not followed."
- An inconsistency is a reason to suspect a problem, not proof; oracles can mislead in both directions.
