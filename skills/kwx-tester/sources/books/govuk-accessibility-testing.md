## Key lessons used in exploratory/accessibility-testing.md

- Services should meet WCAG 2.2 AA, work with common assistive technologies, and be tested with disabled and older users; an audit is needed before public beta.
- Automated tools alone miss issues; tools named: axe, WAVE, ARC Toolkit, SiteImprove.
- Minimum combinations: JAWS with Chrome or Edge, NVDA with Chrome, Firefox or Edge, VoiceOver iOS with Safari, TalkBack with Chrome, Windows Magnifier or Apple Zoom, Dragon with Chrome.
- Magnifier testing at 4x or more; speech recognition: navigate and activate everything by voice.
- Tool audit: 142 planted barriers, 13 tools; best (SortSite) found 40 percent, aXe 29 percent, WAVE 30 percent, lowest 13 percent.
