## Key lessons used in exploratory/i18n-and-localisation-testing.md

- IBM expansion figures: up to 10 characters 200 to 300 percent, 11 to 20 180 to 200, 21 to 30 160 to 180, 31 to 50 140 to 160, over 70 130 percent (the 51 to 70 row looks out of order).
- Vertical expansion for Thai, Arabic, Devanagari, CJK; CJK characters are wider though fewer.
- Let text reflow, avoid fixed width containers, size DB fields for expansion in characters, avoid space saving abbreviations, watch German, Dutch and Finnish compound nouns.
