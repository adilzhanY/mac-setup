# Bauwerk, 30 second commercial

1920 x 1080, 60 fps, 1800 frames. Music at 120 BPM in G major: a beat is 0.5 s (30 frames), a bar is 2 s (120 frames). Every cut and every hit lands on the grid.

Everything on screen is drawn in code: the city is the real LoD2 data around Methfesselstraße 8, the building is the BOo model, the plan is its real ground floor, the cursor is the macOS-White theme from this PC. No screenshots.

Look: Bauwerk's tokens (navy #024e7b, ink #0d2137, AI violet #a855f7), IBM Plex Sans, Lucide icons, glass panels, soft shadows, film grain. Light scenes for finding and working, a dark scene while the AI reads, then light again on the drop.

Copy, in order: "Any address." "In 3D. Instantly." "Reads old floor plans." "Finds every window." "Placed in 3D." "Together. Live." "From G to B." "Report. Ready to send." "Bauwerk" "From survey to iSFP."
