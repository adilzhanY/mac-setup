Sampled instruments (2026-10-29): the API, for updating references/music.md and references/rights.md
=====================================================================================================

Why: the user hears the numpy synthesized instruments as "AI like". The engine's patterns, harmony
and form stay; they are now performed by recorded CC0 instruments instead of oscillators, with a
systematic feel instead of random jitter, and the -24 dB dips are gone. Measured, not listened to.

Files
  sampler.py             the player (numpy, scipy, soundfile only)
  sampler_map.json       engine kinds -> instruments -> SFZ layers, plus the rooms
  fetch_instruments.sh   downloads the library (pinned commits), checks the disk, converts to FLAC,
                         writes SOURCES.txt with the licence of every source
  score.py               uses it unless music.json says "instruments": "synth" (samples are the default
                         when the library exists; without it the synth plays and a line says why)
  demo.py                samples by default; --synth renders a sketch or a --film with the synth

music.json
  "instruments": "samples" | "synth"     default samples; samples falls back to the synth for any kind
                                         the map does not cover and for everything when the library
                                         is missing (it prints why)
  "samples_map": {"keys:piano": "felt"}  per film overrides of the map's kinds (a name, or
                                         {"instrument": ..., "key": ..., "vel": [lo, hi], "db": ...,
                                         "octave": ..., "width": ...})
  "pump"                                 default 0.12 with samples (0.4 synth): players do not duck

Library
  default /games/mgpro/instruments, or $MGPRO_INSTRUMENTS, or "library" in sampler_map.json.
  audio/fetch_instruments.sh [DEST]   about 1.3 GB as FLAC; stops if / < 3 GB or DEST disk < 8 GB free;
                                      re-runnable (skips files present)

sampler.py API
  note_number("F#3") -> 54          c4 = 60
  parse_sfz(path, defines=None) -> (opcode dicts with "_path", control)
  regions_from_sfz(path, switch=None, cc=None, defines=None, chokes=None) -> [Region]
      cc overrides set_cc defaults ({"4": 127}); defines pins $names over the file's own
  load_folder(folder or [files]) -> [Region]    names carry note (C4), layer (v2, vl3, pp..fff,
      soft/med/loud), round robin (rr2); no notes in the names = one unpitched one shot drum on 60
  Instrument(regions, name, seed, releases=True, edges=None)
      .from_path(sfz or folder, name, seed, switch, releases, cc, defines)
      .pick(m, vel, trigger="attack") -> regions that sound (one round robin take, never the same
          take twice in a row on a key and layer; velocity outside every layer takes the nearest)
      .play(region, m, vel, dur, one_shot=None, attack=None, release=None) -> (2, n) float32
      .render(events, seconds=None, pedal=(), vel=(1, 127), gamma=1.0, releases=None) -> (2, n)
          events: {"t": s, "m": midi, "v": 0..1, "d": s, "pan": -1..1, "gain", "attack", "release"}
          pedal: [(down_s, up_s)], note offs inside wait for up
  Layered([(Instrument, lokey, hikey), ...]).render(...)   split by key
  room_ir(rt60, predelay, size, seed, bright) -> stereo IR;  load_ir(path) -> 48 kHz, normalised
  convolve(x, ir, wet, lo=180, hi=9000, width=0.6)          band limited, narrowed wet return
  Performer(seed, overrides, library, map_path, verbose)    what score.py uses
      .available, .has(family, kind), .entry(family, kind)
      .note(bus, family, kind, t, m, v, d, gain, pan, **event) -> False = use the synth
      .chord(bus, family, kind, t, notes, v, d, gain, spread, width, **event)
      .render(buses, seconds, pedal_by_bus)   every part into its dsp.Bus, peak matched per
          instrument and drum key, side narrowed by "width" (default 0.75)
      .space(x, room, wet)                    "drums", "room", "hall" from the map
  CLI: sampler.py --test | --list | --demo keys:felt [--out x.wav]

What the player supports from SFZ
  #include, #define, comments, <control> default_path, set_ccN, note_offset, octave_offset;
  <global>/<master>/<group>/<region>; key, lokey, hikey, pitch_keycenter (numbers or names), lovel,
  hivel, tune, transpose, pitch_keytrack, volume (+ group/master/global_volume), amplitude, pan,
  offset, end, loop_mode=one_shot, ampeg attack/hold/decay/sustain/release, amp_veltrack,
  amp_velcurve_N, xfin/xfout_lovel/hivel, trigger=release with rt_decay, group/off_by/off_mode
  (chokes follow off_by chains, also through regions the controllers leave out), seq_length/
  seq_position and lorand/hirand as round robins, locc/hicc against the controller defaults,
  sw_last against sw_default, *_onccN for pan, volume, envelope and offset (linear).
  Not modelled: filters (fil_type, cutoff), LFOs, pitch envelopes, tune controllers, loop points
  inside files (notes longer than a sample loop its tail with a crossfade instead), legato scripts,
  note_polyphony. A stereo file whose channels are close to polarity inverted (correlation under
  -0.3, some spaced pair notes) plays its louder channel so it survives a mono phone speaker.

Second round (same day, from the user's review: the automatic dips "sounded like the audio broke"
and the music still sounded AI like). These change the synth path too.

1. Dips. "dips" now defaults to 0 (score.py and the kit's src/music.json): no gain dips unless a
   brief asks ("auto" or a list, unchanged otherwise). A form section with "parts": ["dip"] is an
   arranged breath, not a gain hole: drums, bass, hook, fills and the crash stop; the chord keeps
   ringing (one sustained strum when the keys are keyed, else nothing) and the pad plays the chord
   with a short 80 ms attack; reverb tails ring on. The level curve holds the previous section's
   energy through it, and breath_guard(mix, grid) lifts a breath that measures more than 3 dB
   under the bar before it (40 ms ramps, at most 12 dB). plan_dips() no longer turns dip sections
   into gain dips. Measured on the six demos: breaths sit -2.7 to +2.2 dB against the bar before,
   the quietest 50 ms inside any breath about 5 dB under it.
2. Instruments default to "samples" when the library exists (code and kit music.json).
3. Feel (score.Feel) replaces the independent random jitter per note:
   - a timing template per part (kick, snare, hat, perc, bass, chords, hook), drawn once from the
     film's seed and repeated every bar: each step leans late by LEAN x share x humanize_ms (kick
     0.1, snare 0.7, hat 0.6, perc 0.5, bass 0.25, chords 0.45, hook 0.35) plus a fixed deviation,
     half size on beats, clipped to -0.5 to +2 times the share; the bar downbeat is always exact.
     HUMANIZE shares and humanize_ms (default 12) keep their meaning as the size.
   - an accent template per part (bar downbeat 1.06, beats 1.0, offbeat 8ths 0.93, 16ths 0.87,
     times a fixed 3 percent hand per step) and a velocity contour over each phrase (phrase_bars,
     default 4): a rise and fall of 6 percent for drums and bass, 12 for chords, 16 for the hook;
     plus 2 to 3 percent random velocity so sample layers still vary.
   - pushes of 5 to 15 ms early only on the hook's peak note, the last note of the answer phrase
     and the last chord hit of a phrase's cadence bar.
   - the fill roll lost its random jitter; beatless films keep their rubato (hook 0 to 35 ms late,
     strums and bass as before).
   Feel.hits(pattern, t0, part, bar, swing, depth) is grooves.hits on the grid plus the template.
