Sound engine rework (2026-10-27): what changed, for updating references/music.md and references/sound.md
=====================================================================================================

Why: the first films sounded made by the same machine. Measured against 129 professional films
(mgpro-research/research_notes/Sound design in motion graphics/real_films_measured.md) they were
clones (profile distance 0.88 to 1.60, pro p10 2.67; torq and Sendoku shared 42 percent of their
transients), too bright (centroid 2.3 to 3.0 kHz, pro 1.8), too narrow in range (LRA 3.6 to 4.2 LU,
pro 5.8), opened at full level, slammed every cut (18.6x, pro 1.2 to 1.7) and quantized everything.
The synth effects rang 5 to 10 times too long, were pure tones, mostly flat and mono, and one
sound served many events (sfx/ANALYSIS.md). Everything below was measured, not listened to.


1. sfx.py (rewritten)
---------------------
CHARACTERS = glass, wood, metal, plastic, paper, digital, soft (plastic and paper are new).
MAT holds each material's profile: click band, d20, ping and its fall, partials, ring, noise share,
blip fall, width, attack, plus decay (how long hits ring), drop (how far hits fall in pitch) and hw
(width of hits). Characters now differ in envelope, noise, width and pitch movement, not only in
frequency.

Every effect takes seed=None (same seed, same sound) and is peak normalised to v. Most take size
(0 light to 1 heavy: lower, longer, wider). One effect per job:

  tap(character, v, size, seed)                  press: 4 to 15 ms noise, falling
  click(character, v, seed)                      press plus a quieter release 45 to 80 ms later
  tick(character, pitch, v, seed)                data tick, 4 to 9 ms, 3 to 10 kHz
  blip(key, i, character, v, seed, octave=5, ring=None)   select: tone plus noise transient, falls 1 to 2 st
                                                 (CHANGED: the old length argument is gone; octave added)
  pop(key, i, character, v, seed)                soft pop, 740 to 990 Hz, rising 3 to 7 st
  text(key, i, character, v, seed)               text appearing, falls an octave or more
  toggle(key, on, character, v, seed)            click plus two tones a fourth apart, up for on
  card(character, v, size, seed)                 card lands: 80 to 180 ms thud, mostly 120 to 500 Hz
  chip(key, i, character, v, seed)               light object lands: struck tone near C6
  land(character, size, key, i, v, seed)         chip, card or impact by size
  impact(character, v, size, seed, tail=0)       hit: sub body 50 to 70 ms plus a 120 to 500 Hz knock
                                                 (20 to 30 percent of the energy, survives phones)
  boom(character, v, seed)                       impact with a long cinematic sub tail
  swish(seconds=0.12, character, v, seed, direction, whistle)   60 to 160 ms, low air plus whistle
  whoosh(seconds, character, v, seed, weight, air, whistle, direction, rise)
                                                 length follows the motion; up to 0.18 s it is a swish;
                                                 otherwise a wide low "whum" falling 2 st; air=True is
                                                 the bright designed sweep (5 to 8 kHz, wide)
  transition(seconds, character, v, seed, weight)   whoosh into a small landing
  panel(opening, character, v, seconds, seed)    sheet opens (rising sweep, ENDS on frame) or closes
  success(key, character, v, seed, degrees)      4 notes 60 to 80 ms apart, 190 to 290 ms d20
  chime(key, character, v, degrees, seed)        kept, calls success
  error(key, character, v, seed, hits=2)         low, buzzy, semitone clash, falling
  tier(key, i, character, v, seed)               struck hit plus a bell rising per tier
  logo(key, character, v, seed, notes=None)      hook notes then an inharmonic bell, 1.2 to 2.4 s
  riser, reverse_swell (now take seed), sub_drop, stutter, tape_stop, texture(..., seed, rate)
  peak_index(x)                                  where a sound peaks, to put a whoosh peak on its frame

Placement: riser, reverse and panel(opening=True) END on their frame; swish, whoosh and transition
PEAK on their frame; everything else starts on it. score.py does this.

Measured: 665 of 686 measures of 152 renders inside the library ranges of ANALYSIS.md section 4
(sfx_catalog.py --synth-only --check). Before: blip d20 166 to 413 ms (now 19 to 29), tick 19 (now
5 to 7), click 26 to 27 (now 6 to 10; taps 6 to 14) with 77 percent in 120 to 500 Hz (now 0), success 432 to 692
(now 199 to 284), impact 95 to 98 percent sub with d20 179 to 469 (now 70 to 79 percent sub, 20 to
29 percent in 120 to 500 Hz, d20 20 to 47), whoosh one 0.41 s hiss at 1.7 to 7.1 kHz (now swish,
swing at 350 to 400 Hz, airy at 7 to 8 kHz). Pitch: 28 of 43 renders flat before, 58 of 152 now.
Known misses: soft is low passed on purpose (its tick, panel and success sit a little dark); paper
has no tonal chip (it lands as a light card); the text fall reads as half a semitone because pYIN's
first frames sit on zero padding (the frames after show 762 to 511 Hz).


2. vary.py (new): no event plays the same way twice
----------------------------------------------------
  v = Vary(film_seed, pool=4)
  s = v.play(name, make, kind, key=None, pool=None)   make(seed) builds one take
  v.take(name), v.take_seed(name, take), v.cents(kind), v.gain(), v.offset(loose)

A pool of takes per effect (each its own synthesis seed built from the film seed, so two films
never share a transient), served from a shuffled bag with no immediate repeat, then pitch jitter
like a sampler (CENTS: tonal 12, bell 6, impact 60, noisy 120, whoosh 150, sample 100 cents),
level plus or minus 1.5 dB, and a small late leaning start offset only for loose sounds (ambient
cues, textures). Picture synced hits stay frame exact. python vary.py runs its self test.


3. samples.py (new): CC0 library sounds, off by default
-------------------------------------------------------
music.json:  "samples": {"enabled": false, "jobs": ["card", "logo"] or "all", "layer": ["impact"]}
  jobs   played from a sample instead of the synth
  layer  a sample under the synth, at -6 dB
  Samples(seed, film_root, vary, jobs, layer); .wants(job), .play(job), .write_assets()
  family(job) gives a job's files: the picks of sfx/ANALYSIS.md section 3, else the 3 nearest by
  features in the job's category (no clipped files, lead under 150 ms).
  python audio/samples.py lists every job's family.
Files are copied into the film (public/sfx/<pack>__<path>), read from there, their lead silence
trimmed; public/sfx/ASSETS.txt lists file, pack, source page, CC0 1.0. Same variation as synth.


4. score.py
-----------
  score.py [--root FILM_DIR]           default: the folder above audio/
  build(brief, cues, seconds, root=None, verbose=True) -> (master, grid, dips)
  render(brief, cues, seconds) -> (music unlevelled, grid)
  effects(cues, seconds, key, character, vary, samples=None)
  plan_dips(brief, grid, seconds, cuts), dip_curve(dips, n, depth_db), energy_curve(grid, n), report(...)
  JOBS (job: level, variation kind, anchor), ALIASES (old cue names), WEIGHT_GAIN, DEFAULT_WEIGHT, TRANSIENT

Cue fields (cues.json):
  sound      a job (tap click tick blip pop text toggle card chip land impact boom swish whoosh airy
             transition panel_open panel_close success error tier logo riser reverse sub_drop texture)
             or an alias (place block -> chip, word type -> text, switch -> toggle, open panel ->
             panel_open, close -> panel_close, chime done -> success, bell -> logo, hit final ->
             impact, sub -> sub_drop, count grain -> tick, key -> tap). gap and stop are skipped.
  weight     hero | support | ambient. Gains 1.0, 0.4, 0.16; about a third of ambient events play
             nothing. Defaults: logo and boom hero, texture and tick ambient, the rest support. Only
             hero impacts keep full size and the long sub. More than one hero per 8 s is reported.
  cut        true marks a picture cut. A support cue with a hard attack on a cut drops to ambient
             (the music carries ordinary cuts); automatic dips stay off cuts; fingerprint measures
             the rise at these cuts.
  size       0 to 1, or "light" / "heavy"
  material   overrides the film's character for this cue
  seconds, index, on (toggle), notes (logo hook MIDI notes), air (whoosh), direction (1 or -1),
  pan, loose (allow a small start offset)

Form (music.json "form"):
  "parts": ["dip"]   NEW: the section is a dip: the previous section keeps playing 24 dB down
                     (not dead air). Use this for the gap before a drop. Keep it near 0.3 s (an
                     eighth of a bar at 105 BPM): a 0.57 s gap made the hero cut read 27x.
  "silence"          still true silence; professional films rarely use it (71 percent have none).
  "hero": true       the section that gets the crash (default: the highest energy section only).
  "crash" now plays only on the hero section.

New music.json fields (all optional, defaults in brackets):
  beat          [true]   false: no drums, one long bass note per chord, rubato strums and hook,
                         swells instead of crashes, a stronger texture bed (29 percent of pro films
                         have no clear pulse; measured pulse 0.03 to 0.06)
  humanize_ms   [12]     median timing offset of music onsets; per part shares in HUMANIZE (kick
                         0.3, snare 0.55, hat and perc 1.0, bass 0.45, chords 0.8, hook 0.7), late
                         leaning, the bar's kick downbeat exact, velocity spread 6 percent
  opening       ["soft"] the first second about 2 LU under the whole; "cold" opens at full level
  opening_lu    [seeded -1.6 to -3.5]   target of the soft opening
  head          [seeded 0 to 0.12 s]    silence before the first sound
  dips          ["auto"] dip sections, then a breath in the opening, one before the end card, one
                         before the loudest section (the main re-entry: a bar long slide down 5 to 8
                         dB before it, then back about 9 dB louder, first hit 20 to 70 ms after),
                         more at bar lines, about one per 7 s, off cuts, never at 45.8 percent (where
                         all three AI films had their gap). Or a list: {"at": "drop"} or
                         {"bar": 6.5, "len": 0.3, "main": false}. 0 turns them off.
  dip_db        [-24]
  lra           [6.0]    the level span between energy 0 and 1 is fitted to land this LRA
  tone          [{"centroid": 1800, "rolloff": 4000}]   false keeps the mix's own brightness
  bass_harmonics [0.3]   harmonics of the low end added for small speakers
  bed           [0.5, beatless 1.5]   quiet off grid percussive grains between the hits (rule 12)
  bed_rate      [7, beatless 10]      grains per second at energy 0.5
  keep_alive    [true]   drumless bars between 55 and 85 percent of the film get the lightest part
  pool          [4]      takes per effect
  samples       [enabled false]       see section 3
  fx_level      [0.5]    unchanged
The kit example (src/music.json) now carries these fields, and its gap is a 0.125 bar dip.

score.py prints LRA, opening, centroid, rolloff, grid offset, pulse, density at 75 to 85 percent,
planned and measured dips, then RMS per section. A dip section reads about -30 dB RMS, not -50.


5. dsp.py
---------
  dec(n, d20_ms, a_ms=0.5, hold_ms=0)   envelope by the part you hear: 20 dB down d20_ms after the peak
                                        (perc() still takes a TIME CONSTANT: 20 dB down at 2.3 x length)
  glide(f0, f1, n, ms, curve)           pitch tracks for falls, pops, dopplers
  pink(n, r), modal(n, freqs, d20s, amps, r, detune), repitch(x, cents), spread(a, b, width)
  harmonics(x, cut=120, amount=0.3)     low end saturated, only its harmonics added back
  darken(x, centroid=1800, rolloff=4000)   shelves toward the pro medians; lifts a mix under 60 percent
  master(mix, lufs=-14, tp=-1, tone=(1800, 4000), bass_harmonics=0.3)
        CHANGED: gain stages the mix to lufs - 8 before the glue compressor (threshold lufs - 2). The
        old fixed -16 dBFS threshold on an unstaged mix squashed 3.6 LU of range and lifted openings.
  balance() default top range is now 0.1 to 1.5 percent above 8 kHz (was 3 to 10, which brightened)


6. grooves.hits
---------------
  hits(pattern, start, step, swing, steps_per_beat, humanize, rng, lock=(), late=0.3, vel=0.06)
  humanize is a spread in seconds leaning late by late x humanize; steps in lock stay exact;
  velocity varies by vel whenever rng is given.


7. fingerprint.py, filmstats.py, pro_stats.json
-----------------------------------------------
  fingerprint.py film.wav --bpm B --against ~/.claude/skills/mgpro/music/registry.json \
      [--cues src/cues.json] [--music src/music.json] [--video out/film.mp4] [--cold-open]
  fingerprint.py film.wav --checks [--cues ...]        pro range checks without a registry
  fingerprint.py --build-stats ~/dev/mgpro-research/films   rebuilds pro_stats.json
Prints pass or FAIL per check with the professional range beside it; exits 1 on any failure:
  profile distance  10 trait profile (tempo, onset density, percussive share, share under 120 Hz,
                    LRA, loudest 3 s position, grid offset, side share, dips per 10 s, density
                    variation), standardized as crossfilm.py does; at least 2.67 (pro p10, different
                    makers) from EVERY registry film
  shared transients at most 10 percent against every registry film (pro median 4.6)
  centroid          982 to 2652 Hz;   rolloff 1714 to 5927 Hz
  LRA               5 to 11.4 LU
  opening           first second at most -1.0 LU against the whole (skip with --cold-open)
  rise at cuts      at most 3.93x (pro p90; median 1.71); cuts are cues with "cut": true, else hero
                    cues, else section starts (--music), or scene cuts of a video (--video). Also
                    printed: hero and ordinary cuts apart, and what this music's bar downbeats
                    measure with no cut at all.
  informational     grid offset, onsets on grid, dips per 10 s, pulse, density, percussive share,
                    side share, tempo, each dip, the 2 s density curve
Registry films are measured once and cached in ~/.cache/mgpro/filmstats/.
filmstats.py: analyze(wav or array, sr, patches=True), loudness, tone, cut_rise, shared_transients,
profile_vector, video_cuts, pro_stats(). It is a port of the research analyze.py and cutsync.py.


8. sfx_catalog.py, demo.py
--------------------------
  sfx_catalog.py --synth-only --check   renders every effect in every character, writes
                                        sfx/synth.json, prints each against TARGETS (no library

9. Facts the references should carry
------------------------------------
- Cuts on bar downbeats measure 3 to 9x from the music alone, with no effect. Keep downbeat cuts
  for structure (act changes, the reveal, the end card); let ordinary cuts fall with the action.
  Professional cuts are barely more aligned with musical hits than chance (72 vs 64 percent).
- Mark cues with weight and cut. Hero: the title, the reveal, the end card. Everything else small.
- The old guidance "gap and stop sections should read below -50 dB" and "dead stops" is replaced by
  dips; true silence at most once or twice.
- sound.md's effect recipes (click with a 380 Hz body, the 0.41 s whoosh, sub only impacts) describe
  the old synth.
- The checks are measured, not heard. Nothing in this rework was listened to.


10. Found recordings and story sounds (mgpro 3.1.0, 2026-10-31)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cue fields, for premade sounds found per film (references/sfx-selection.md):
  file        a recording, or a list of recordings rotated without an immediate repeat; looked up
              in the film's public/ folder, then the CC0 library (~/.local/share/mgpro/sfx); the
              silence before the first transient is cut (a 123 ms lead made a click land 7 frames
              late), the peak normalised, the tail faded 5 ms
  story       true: an action the story turns on; never ambient, never randomly skipped, high
              passed at 170 Hz ("hp" changes it), and the music ducks under it
  gain        a per cue multiplier
music.json:
  story_duck_db   [3.0]  how far the music steps aside under story cues (20 ms early, about 150 ms)
Every found library file a cue played is copied into public/sfx/ with a licence line in
public/sfx/ASSETS.txt (copy_found). Measure story sounds with the script in
references/sfx-selection.md, section 11; the master limiter caps transient loudness, so past a
point gain does nothing and the duck and a fuller recording are the levers.
