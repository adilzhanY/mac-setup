# Editors and pro tools: reading list

Sources read for the `editors/` folder. Marked **[read]** when the text was actually fetched and read for this skill, **[secondary]** when only summaries, reviews or forum threads were available. Notes on papers and talks are in `../books/`.

## Papers, books and talks (notes in ../books/)

- [read] Shneiderman, "Direct Manipulation: A Step Beyond Programming Languages", IEEE Computer 1983. https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~pr10/iui/shneiderman83.pdf (note: `shneiderman-1983-direct-manipulation.md`)
- [read] Kurtenbach and Buxton, "The Limits of Expert Performance Using Hierarchic Marking Menus", InterCHI 1993. https://www.billbuxton.com/MMExpert.html (note: `kurtenbach-buxton-1993-hierarchic-marking-menus.md`)
- [read] Kurtenbach and Buxton, "User Learning and Performance with Marking Menus", CHI 1994. https://billbuxton.com/MMUserLearn.html (note: `kurtenbach-buxton-1994-user-learning-marking-menus.md`)
- [read, partial] Raskin, The Humane Interface, via Brown course notes and the Raskin Center principles. https://cs.brown.edu/courses/csci0920/2005/bell.raskin.pdf and https://www.raskincenter.org/rchi/core-principles (note: `the-humane-interface.md`, written by the intuitive-design research thread; the editor lessons are in `editors/tools-modes-selection.md`)
- [read] Bret Victor, "Inventing on Principle", CUSEC 2012, transcript. https://jamesclear.com/great-speeches/inventing-on-principle-by-bret-victor (note: `victor-2012-inventing-on-principle.md`)
- [secondary] Tesler, "A Personal History of Modeless Text Editing and Cut/Copy-Paste", ACM Interactions 2012 (blocked, 403). https://interactions.acm.org/archive/view/july-august-2012/a-personal-history-of-modeless-text-editing-and-cut-copy-paste ; used Wikipedia "Mode (user interface)" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mode_(user_interface) and "Larry Tesler" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Tesler instead.
- [secondary] Nielsen, response time limits (0.1 s, 1 s, 10 s), from Usability Engineering (1993), via https://www.nngroup.com/videos/3-response-time-limits-interaction-design/ and quoting posts.
