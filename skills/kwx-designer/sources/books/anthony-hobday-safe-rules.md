# Anthony Hobday: visual design rules you can safely follow every time

- **Who:** independent product designer who writes and curates design reading lists (design books he recommends, short design books, every visual design and interaction design book, the best WWDC design videos).
- **Status:** free.
- **Legal sources:** https://anthonyhobday.com/sideprojects/saferules/ ; reading lists at https://anthonyhobday.com/blog/20260408 ; WWDC list at https://anthonyhobday.com/blog/20220614
- **Read:** all three pages, October 2026.

## The 28 rules (any can be broken with a good reason)

1. Near-black and near-white instead of pure black and white.
2. Saturate neutrals slightly with the interface colour, under 5% saturation in HSB.
3. High contrast for important elements; structure and shadows as little contrast as possible.
4. Be deliberate about everything: whitespace, alignment, size, spacing, colour, shadows.
5. Optical alignment over mathematical alignment.
6. Adjust text spacing to size: large text tighter letter spacing and line height, small text looser.
7. A container border contrasts with both the container and the background (lighter than both on dark, darker than both on light).
8. Align every element with something else.
9. Palette colours with distinct brightness values.
10. Warm or cool neutrals, not both.
11. Base measurements on a scale (multiples of a unit such as 8).
12. Arrange elements by visual weight, heaviest on the outside edge.
13. A 12 column grid for horizontal layouts (divides into 1, 2, 3, 4).
14. Measure spacing between points of high contrast.
15. Closer elements are lighter, in light and dark modes.
16. Drop shadow blur = 2x offset (4px offset, 8px blur); closer elements can use lower shadow opacity.
17. Pair complex with simple; never complex on complex.
18. Container brightness close to the background: within about 12% (HSB) in dark interfaces, 7% in light (from reviewing about 100 well designed sites).
19. Outer padding equal to or greater than inner padding.
20. Body text 16px or larger.
21. Line length around 70 characters (60 to 80 fine).
22. Button horizontal padding twice the vertical.
23. No more than two typefaces.
24. Nested corner radius = outer radius minus the gap.
25. Never two hard divides side by side (background change, container edge, divider line).
26. Avoid shadows in dark interfaces.
27. One depth technique throughout.
28. Lower the contrast of icons paired with text.

## Reading lists (summary)

- **Recommended:** Don't Make Me Think (Krug), On Web Typography (Santa Maria), Designing Visual Interfaces (Mullet and Sano), The Non-Designer's Design Book (Williams), Just Enough Research (Hall).
- **Short books (200 pages or fewer) recommended:** also The Shape of Design (Chimero), The Vignelli Canon, Less But Better (Rams), Envisioning Information (Tufte), Laws of UX (Yablonski), UI Design for Programmers (Spolsky), Accessibility for Everyone (Kalbag), Simplicity: A Matter of Design (Mollerup), Picture This (Bang), and others.
